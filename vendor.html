What is a vendor?

The person who always has the currency.

Imagine Ada needs $100 and has naira. You have dollars sitting ready. She pays you the naira, you send her the $100 — done in minutes. That is all a vendor does: keep foreign currency ready, and swap it fast when an order comes.

Trex brings you the customers, shows your rating, and guarantees every trade with your bond. You bring the currency and the speed.

THE CORE RULE — 50% PER-TRADE BOND

For every single order, you lock 50% of that order's value as a bond before the trade starts.

Customer orders $200 → you lock $100 for that trade.
You deliver → your $100 comes straight back, plus your earnings.
You fail to deliver → the $100 compensates the customer.

No standing balance. No exceptions. Every trade stands on its own bond — that is what makes customers trust a stranger with their money.

How vending works, step by step

1. Post offers. Pick your pairs, rate and limits. Buyers find you instantly.

2. Lock 50%. Each order locks half its value as bond automatically.

3. Confirm & deliver fast. Confirm their payment, send the currency, attach proof.

4. Get it all back. Bond released the moment the trade completes. Ratings build your name.

Do's and Don'ts — every vendor lives by these
✅ Always do
  • Keep the currencies you offer actually available
  • Confirm payments within minutes, day and night
  • Attach proof for every delivery
  • Keep 50% bond cover ready for each order you accept
  • Talk in the trade chat — it protects you too
❌ Never do
  • Accept orders you cannot fill right now
  • Ask customers to pay outside Trex
  • Deliver late or in a different amount
  • Share your login or let others vend on your name
  • Dispute honestly completed trades
NOT FOR EVERYONE

Vending is only for people who are 100% capable of delivering currency swaps — you hold real balances, you respond in minutes, and you accept the 50% bond on every order. If you only want to exchange your own money sometimes, stay a customer — trading needs no bond at all.

What you earn

Your spread on every swap, a public rating that brings more orders, and higher limits as your record grows. Higher limits and more orders follow as your record grows.
